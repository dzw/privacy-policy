一、中文

1. 简介

本隐私政策说明“字幕搜索下载器 / Subtitle Hunter”（以下简称“本应用”）如何处理你的信息。本应用由开发者（以下简称“我们”）提供。使用本应用即表示你理解并同意本政策。

本应用无需注册或登录。我们不会主动收集你的姓名、邮箱、电话号码、通讯录、照片、精确位置或支付信息。

2. 我们收集和使用的数据

  • 搜索关键词：你在搜索框输入的片名、文件名或关键词，会被发送到第三方字幕服务，用于查找匹配字幕。该关键词仅用于完成你发起的检索请求。
  • 字幕下载内容：你下载的字幕文件只保存在你设备的下载目录中。我们不会上传你下载的字幕文件。
  • 广告相关数据：本应用集成 Google AdMob 横幅广告。AdMob 及其广告合作伙伴可能收集广告标识符、IP 地址、设备信息、应用互动和广告互动数据，用于广告投放、频次控制、反欺诈和效果衡量。

3. 数据分享

我们不会出售你的个人信息。我们会在以下范围内分享数据：

  • 字幕服务提供方：为完成字幕搜索与下载，搜索关键词和下载请求会发送至第三方字幕接口（包括 api-shoulei-ssl.xunlei.com 与 subtitle.v.geilijiasu.com）。
  • Google AdMob 及其广告合作伙伴：用于在本应用展示横幅广告及进行广告效果衡量。
  • 法律要求：当法律法规要求或为保护我们、用户或公众的合法权益时，我们可能依法披露相关信息。

4. 数据保留

  • 搜索关键词仅在请求过程中传输，本应用自身不保存搜索历史。
  • 下载的字幕文件保留在你的设备上，直到你手动删除或卸载应用。
  • 广告相关数据的保留期限由 Google 及其广告合作伙伴按其隐私政策执行。

5. 安全

本应用的字幕搜索、字幕下载和广告请求均通过 HTTPS 传输。我们采取合理措施保护数据安全，但互联网传输无法保证绝对安全。

6. 你的选择

  • 你可以选择不输入任何搜索关键词，但这样无法使用字幕搜索功能。
  • 你可以在 Android 系统设置中重置或限制广告标识符、选择退出个性化广告。
  • 你可以随时手动删除设备下载目录中的字幕文件。
  • 如需查询、更正或删除我们持有的与你相关的数据，请通过下方联系方式联系我们。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用并非面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会明知收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。如果你认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方联系方式联系我们。

字幕内容来自第三方公开服务。你应自行确认下载和使用字幕的版权及当地法律要求。

English

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Subtitle Hunter (package name: com.kwill.subtitlehunter, "the App") handles your information. The App is provided by the developer ("we", "us"). By using the App, you understand and agree to this Policy.

The App does not require registration or sign-in. We do not actively collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

2. Data we collect and use

  • Search keywords: The movie title, filename, or keyword you enter is sent to a third-party subtitle service to find matching subtitles. It is used only to complete your search request.
  • Downloaded subtitles: Subtitle files you download are saved only to your device's Downloads folder. We do not upload your downloaded subtitle files.
  • Ad-related data: The App includes Google AdMob banner ads. AdMob and its partners may collect advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, app interactions, and ad interactions for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with:

  • Google AdMob and its advertising partners: For showing banner ads and measuring ad performance.
  • Legal authorities: When required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of users, the public, or us.

4. Data retention

  • Search keywords are transmitted only during the request. The App itself does not store your search history.
  • Downloaded subtitle files remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • Ad-related data is retained by Google and its partners according to their privacy policies.

5. Security

Subtitle search, subtitle downloads, and ad requests are transmitted over HTTPS. We use reasonable measures to protect data, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices

  • You may choose not to enter any search keyword, but then the subtitle search feature cannot be used.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in Android settings.
  • You can delete downloaded subtitle files from your device at any time.
  • To request access, correction, or deletion of data we hold about you, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Third-party policies
